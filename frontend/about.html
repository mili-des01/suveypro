Our Story

SurveyPro began with a simple conviction: property decisions deserve measurements that hold up — in court, at the county office, and on the construction site.

What started as a two-person field crew has grown into a full surveying practice serving homeowners, attorneys, developers, contractors and public agencies. We have completed more than 500 projects across residential, commercial and infrastructure sectors.

We invest continuously in our equipment and our people — GNSS receivers, robotic total stations, UAV photogrammetry and rigorous continuing education for every licensed surveyor on staff.
