Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our surveys, process and deliverables.

How long does a survey take?
Most residential surveys are completed within 5–10 business days. Larger topographical or construction projects depend on site size and complexity — we confirm a timeline with every quote.
How much does a survey cost?
Pricing depends on the survey type, property size, terrain and records research required. After you submit a booking request, we provide a fixed quote before any work begins — no surprises.
What deliverables will I receive?
Depending on the service: a certified survey plat, digital terrain models, CAD files, staking reports or a full written survey report. All deliverables are prepared for county submission and lender requirements.
Do I need to be present during the survey?
Generally no — as long as our crew can access the property. If gates, dogs or interior access are involved, we coordinate with you in advance.
Will you mark the property corners?
Yes. Boundary surveys include setting or verifying permanent monuments at each corner, and we flag them for easy identification.
Which areas do you serve?
We serve the greater Austin metro area and surrounding counties, with project capability across Texas. Large projects outside our normal area are considered case by case.
Can you re-survey a boundary that is in dispute?
Yes — boundary resolution is one of our core services. We recover original monuments, research historical plats, and produce certified documentation suitable for legal proceedings.
